      About

      Lettering defaults to the community-standard Speffz scheme (A–X for edges, A–X for corners); Settings → Letter scheme holds your own overrides. Everything is stored locally in your browser — no account, no server. See README for the full scheme tables, deploy notes, and roadmap.
